## Assess Google Business Profile eligibility

1. Confirm that customers can visit a qualifying, staffed pickup location during published hours and that the owner has permission to represent it. If an address is displayed, Google requires permanent fixed signage and a real customer-facing location. A private or residential pickup arrangement needs case-specific review with Google before listing. [Google Business Profile guidelines](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177)
2. Service-area profiles describe businesses that actually visit or deliver to customers. Pickup-only fulfillment does not establish eligibility for a hidden-address service-area listing. If the location fails the storefront conditions and the bakery does not travel to customers, continue with organic search and authentic community mentions.
3. If eligible and approved, create or claim one listing under **Salted Focaccia**. Choose **Bakery** if available and accurate. Set the canonical URL, approved address visibility, actual customer-facing hours, a verified contact channel, original bakery photos and the Instagram profile where supported. Reflect closures with special hours where appropriate. Seek genuine customer reviews through the bakery’s normal customer relationship.
4. The website uses Organization markup until an approved public address is also present visibly on Pickup. Google's LocalBusiness search feature requires the physical address. A schema type by itself does not establish Maps or Business Profile eligibility. [Google LocalBusiness structured data](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/local-business) · [Schema.org Bakery](https://schema.org/Bakery)
